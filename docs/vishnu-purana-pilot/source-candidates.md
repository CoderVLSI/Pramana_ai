# Vishnu Purana source candidates — research only

Checked 2026-10-16. No transcription, scripture text, scanned pages or OCR XML is committed here; no Vishnu Purana data was uploaded to Supabase. Downloads and working images stayed outside Git. Only archive.org printed scans were examined as potential text sources. Catalogue/biographical websites below supply metadata, not scripture text.

## Recommendation and evidence limits

For Sanskrit, **provisionally prefer the 1882 Calcutta Jivananda edition (J)**: larger, vertically separated root text is easier to read than the Bombay pothi. Use **R**, the different Ratnagarbha-commentary edition, as a second printed witness after resolving its contributors’ rights. **C is a backup scan of J’s same physical copy, not a third independent edition.** These are the best three *scans* audited here, representing only two editions. A genuinely independent third pre-1931 Devanagari edition, preferably without commentary, remains unfound. None of these is verse-only; Book IV includes substantial prose.

For English, prefer **A, Wilson’s original 1840 John Murray edition**, over the darker thresholded E scan. All six books and the final index are present in both. Do not import a modern online transcription or modern reprint’s added introduction. This research does not authorize publication of any new Sanskrit edition: contributors’ death dates and gap-free page coverage remain open.

**CONFIRMED** below means agreement of two independent sources on the particular fact, not blanket certification of a scan. Primary page observations and single catalogue assertions are labelled as such. Matching derivatives, authority mirrors and uploader labels do not count as independent corroboration. Completeness here distinguishes six-book structural coverage from a leaf-by-leaf/verse-by-verse audit; the latter was not performed.

## Index convention and reproducibility

`n` is the **zero-based image index**, not a PDF page number, printed page number, or folio. Links below use `/page/nN/mode/1up`; the corresponding inspected JPEG is `https://archive.org/download/ITEM/page/nN_w1600.jpg` (some initial samples used width 1200). One image is one page/folio side in these items. Folios have two sides. Do not use IA’s inferred page labels as printed evidence.

The exact `_djvu.xml` byte streams were downloaded and SHA-256 hashed, and their OBJECT counts compared with scandata. OCR was used only to locate candidate headings; visual inspection supplied the page observations. A hash identifies the downloaded derivative, not the accuracy of its OCR or the completeness of the printed work. IA can regenerate derivatives.

## Rights rule sources and scope

- [Cornell’s copyright term chart, updated 1 January 2026](https://guides.library.cornell.edu/copyright/publicdomain): works published abroad before 1931 generally have expired US terms. Unknown contributor identity/death dates prevent upgrading the Sanskrit editions’ Indian rights status. [Official Act PDF](https://copyright.gov.in/documents/copyright_act_1957.pdf).
