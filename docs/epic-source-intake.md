## Valmiki Ramayana

The official catalogue lists Hindi parts **75 and 76**, and their combined set **CB005**. It separately lists code **583**, Sanskrit shlokas; codes **77** and **1338** also carry Hindi Valmiki Ramayana titles but need copy inspection to determine format and coverage. Catalogue Hindi language tags alone do not establish that a particular scan preserves Sanskrit originals.

Preferred inspection leads are [part 1](https://archive.org/details/valmiki-ramayan-part-1-gita-press) and [part 2](https://archive.org/details/valmiki-ramayan-part-2-gita-press). Their metadata lists page-aware OCR assets of 40,645,919 and 40,872,311 bytes respectively. A [second part-2 lead](https://archive.org/details/fdag_shrimad-valmiki-ramayan-with-hindi-trans.-part-2-series-no.-76-gita-press) explicitly names series code 76 in its upload title, but this is not independently verified publisher metadata. Its uploader language is Sanskrit despite the title mentioning Hindi translation; inspection must resolve actual language coverage.

Release checks must map all seven edition divisions: Bala, Ayodhya, Aranya, Kishkindha, Sundara, Yuddha and Uttara, then sarga/chapter and verse identifiers. Match both volumes' printing, publisher, translator and numbering before combining. The chosen Gita Press edition is a named reference; do not describe it as a universal text identical to every manuscript tradition.

Reject substitutions: Tulsidas's **Ramcharitmanas** is a different authored work, **Adhyatma Ramayana** is a different text, and **Katha Sudha Sagar**, selected Sundarakanda publications, Sharanagati Rahasya and Kalyan Sankshipt issues do not prove complete Valmiki coverage. Internet Archive search produced 67 metadata matches; these are discovery hits, not 67 verified editions.

## Vyasa-attributed Mahabharata

Official Hindi catalogue volumes are **32–37**, with six-volume set **728**. Recorded titles identify:

| Volume/code | Catalogue-title coverage |
|---|---|
| 1 / 32 | Adi and Sabha |
| 2 / 33 | Vana and Virata |
| 3 / 34 | Udyoga and Bhishma |
| 4 / 35 | Title does not enumerate parvas; copy inspection required |
| 5 / 36 | Shanti |
| 6 / 37 | Anushasana, Ashvamedhika, Ashramavasika, Mausala, Mahaprasthanika and Svargarohana |

All eighteen parvas must be mapped. In particular Drona, Karna, Shalya, Sauptika and Stri cannot be treated as covered merely from the vague volume-4 catalogue title. Harivamsha code **38** is an explicitly labelled supplement; preserve its separate identity rather than substituting it for a missing main parva. Codes **39/511** and set **CB011** are explicitly abridged.

Preferred metadata lead: [Mahabharata, Hindi and Sanskrit](https://archive.org/details/mahabharata-by-gita-press-in-hindi-and-sanskrit). It contains separately named PDF/OCR assets for volumes 1–6, each OCR asset approximately 48–71 MB. Having six filenames does not prove six complete scans or matching printings. The existing single-asset private importer selects the first XML only; it must gain per-asset intake and unique volume identifiers before this item can be processed as a six-volume source.

Alternative [combined unabridged-title set](https://archive.org/details/unabridged-mahabharata-6-volumes-set-in-hindi-by-veda-vyasa_202412) has a 342,525,657-byte OCR asset, above the present 150 MB intake limit. [Another six-khandas-title lead](https://archive.org/details/EQlZ_mahabharat-6-khandas-with-translation-and-illustration-by-ramnarayan-datt-pandey) has a much smaller 61,806,812-byte OCR asset; its title alone does not prove that the scan actually contains all six volumes. [Volume-4 alternative](https://archive.org/details/ivzc-mahabharat-chaturth-khand-by-vedavyas-sanskrit-and) explicitly says **Missing Pages**; do not select it for complete coverage without replacement assets.

Internet Archive search returned 135 metadata matches and the first 80 were inspected as discovery titles. Monthly journal issues, character essays, Vidura Niti/Anugita excerpts, indexes, recitation audio, Harivamsha and Jaiminiya Ashvamedha material are not substitutes for the complete eighteen-parva corpus. Do not silently substitute K. M. Ganguli or another publisher under the Gita Press label.
