# Epics and four Vedas

The app's collection selector and library now include Valmiki Ramayana, Mahabharata traditionally attributed to Vyasa, Rigveda, Yajurveda, Samaveda and Atharvaveda. They are pending source collections, not ready scripture corpora. The source register includes 18 Mahapuranas, two epics and four Vedas, with zero approved passages.

Publisher preference remains Gita Press, Gorakhpur. For Ramayana, do not substitute Ramcharitmanas or Adhyatma Ramayana. For Mahabharata, match the exact Sanskrit/Hindi volume set and all eighteen parvas; keep Harivamsha supplemental. "Authentic" is not a universal edition label: disclose the actual reviewed textual witness and edition.

Vedic sources require explicit shakha/recension and edition identity, edition-specific mantra locators and preserved Sanskrit accent marks. Yajurveda's Shukla/Krishna traditions must not be merged without identifying the chosen texts. Samaveda's chant tradition is distinct from ordinary text-to-speech; no general voice model should be described as a verified Vedic recitation. Selected hymns, Upanishads and Veda commentary do not satisfy complete Samhita coverage. Full Gita Press editions are not established by adding targets to the UI.
