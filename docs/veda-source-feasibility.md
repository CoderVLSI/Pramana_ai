## Required choices before intake

| Planned target | Edition detail still required | Citation hierarchy to preserve |
|---|---|---|
| Rigveda | Exact shakha/recension, publisher, Sanskrit text and translation edition | Mandala, sukta, mantra; record alternate ashtaka scheme separately if the edition uses it |
| Yajurveda | Shukla versus Krishna must be explicit; then exact recension such as Madhyandina/Kanva or Taittiriya, not merged as one undifferentiated text | Edition-specific adhyaya/mantra or kanda/prapathaka/anuvaka/mantra |
| Samaveda | Exact shakha such as Kauthuma, Ranayaniya or Jaiminiya, and which text layers are included | Edition-specific divisions and mantra numbers; avoid conflating textual collection with musical notation |
| Atharvaveda | Exact recension such as Shaunaka or Paippalada | Edition-specific kanda/sukta/mantra and variant numbering |

These examples identify the kind of choice needed; they do not select a recension on the user's behalf. Preserve Vedic accents where supplied and assess accent-sensitive OCR separately. Samhita, Brahmana, Aranyaka and Upanishad layers should be declared, not conflated under a coverage claim.
