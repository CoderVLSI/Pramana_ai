# Separate Muktika 108 Upanishads collection

The register in `packages/corpus-schema/upanishads.ts` contains 108 unique work identifiers in Muktika order. This is a named traditional list, not a universal authenticity certificate or a claim that all 108 are currently available from Gita Press. It is separate from the planned four Veda Samhita collections. No scripture downloaded, translated or approved during this metadata task.

Names/order were checked against [Sanskrit Documents’ Muktika list](https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_upanishhat/upanishad_list.html) and independently against [the Muktika list on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muktik%C4%81). Only name metadata is used; these sites are not substituted for the user's requested Gita Press scripture editions.

## Names and variants

Stable identifiers distinguish Jabala (13) from Jabali (104), Maitreya (29) from Maitrayaniya (24), and Atmabodha (42), Atma (76), Adhyatma (73) and Atma-related authored treatises. Ordinal is collection metadata, not a scripture verse number. Source spellings differ: 65 Sannyasa/Brihat-Sannyasa; 67 Akshamalika/Malika; 83 Katharudra/Kathashruti; 87 Bhasmajabala/Bhasma; 88 Rudrakshajabala/Rudraksha; 95 Gopalatapaniya/Gopala Tapani. Display names normalize those variants without creating extra targets. Naradaparivrajaka (43) and Paramahamsaparivrajaka (66) remain separate. Skanda, Varaha and Garuda Upanishads must not merge into the similarly named Puranas.

## Intake requirements

Each target currently has edition, completeness, rights and review pending, with zero indexed passages. Before use, identify a Gita Press publication/printing containing the work, distinguish Sanskrit text from translation/commentary, inspect its contents/title/copyright pages, and map its own section/mantra numbering. Some texts are prose or use chapter/khanda numbering; the register's section/mantra hierarchy is provisional and must be refined per edition. Preserve each work's citation identity even when several share a physical volume.

Public availability, inclusion in a traditional list and an uploader's license declaration do not independently establish edition identity or reuse permissions. Publisher gaps should remain explicit; widening publisher scope requires the user's choice rather than relabelling another source as Gita Press.
