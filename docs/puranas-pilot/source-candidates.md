## Method, scope and evidence strength

- Scripture source candidates are old printed scans on archive.org. No scripture wording is reproduced here, and no scripture text, OCR or page images are committed. No publishing or database operation was performed for this research job.
- Archive metadata and search results were used for discovery; title pages and representative interior/end images were inspected separately. Metadata `date`, modern acquisition stamps, handwritten dates and upload dates are not equivalent to the printed imprint. Specific disagreements are retained below.
- Counts are **scandata image leaves**, including blanks, wrappers, indices and library inserts, not printed pages or verse counts. One pothi image can contain a folio side or multiple photographed pages. Counts do not establish completeness.
- `n` means zero-based image index in the selected scan. Links are page-level provenance anchors, **not a verified whole-book offset**. No global offset is asserted for reset pagination or composite bindings.
- This is a sample-based source survey, not a leaf-by-leaf collation. Opening/middle/end samples establish readability and observed coverage only. Unseen leaves, missing gatherings, exact last chapter/verse and contributor identities remain open unless specifically recorded.
- Excluded as text sources: Gita Press, GRETIL, Sanskrit Documents, Wikisource, modern translations and modern reprint additions. Search-engine leads mentioning those sites were not copied as scripture. Project Gutenberg #7169 was checked for **bibliographic scope only**, not used for text. IA's CC0/PD statements do not independently clear underlying editorial contributions; platform marks and library material are outside any proposed transcription.
- **CONFIRMED** means two independent sources agree on that specific fact. A scan is one primary witness. Catalogue mirrors and multiple articles by the same biographer do not create independent witnesses. “Observed” means directly read in one scan; **UNCONFIRMED** means a missing second authority or unresolved attribution/date.

## Rights framework and shared contributor evidence

US: the standard published-book term puts works published in **1930 or earlier** outside copyright by 2026. This applies to the original published contribution, not new introductions or editorial matter in a modern reprint. See [US Copyright Office, duration](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ15a.pdf). A missing/illegible imprint is not cured by an uploader's early date.

India: [Copyright Act, chapter V, sections 22–24](https://copyright.gov.in/Copyright_Act_1957/chapter_v.html) gives life plus 60 years from the next calendar year for ordinary lifetime publications, the last surviving author for joint works, and qualified rules for anonymous/pseudonymous and posthumous publications. A named contributor whose death is unknown is not automatically an anonymous work. Ancient root text, a translator's work, commentary, critical apparatus and a modern editor's additions need separate treatment.
