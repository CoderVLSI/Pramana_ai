# Vayu Purana (Sanskrit) — Chapter 1 pilot: two blind readings of one scan — NOT FOR PUBLICATION

Source: *Vāyupurāṇam*, ed. Rajendralala Mitra, Bibliotheca Indica, Calcutta (about 1880; exact printed date UNCONFIRMED per ../source-candidates.md), archive.org `b30094343_0001` (volume 1), chapter 1 (the Anukramaṇikā) on images n14–n32. Mitra d. 1891 (CONFIRMED, two sources). Only one edition is available, so the check is two blind readers of the same scan plus an arbiter who looks at the page image for every difference; this catches careless reading errors but cannot catch damage in the print itself.
`vayu_ch1_A.json` (189 entries: 50 high / 109 medium / 30 low) and `vayu_ch1_B.json` (188 entries: 17 high / 140 medium / 31 low), both with verses 1–185 and no number missing. Whole-chapter comparison (spaces, dandas and digits ignored): 98.9% of letters agree; 119 of 189 verses identical; 70 verses with 135 differing spots (`arbiter_input_vayu1.json`). Both readers note that the font prints क्ष and श like other letters and that ८/९ look alike; closing numbers were taken from the sequence in places. Arbiter pass in progress. Vayu is not in `MAHAPURANA_TARGETS` (Shiva is): adding it to the register is an owner decision.
