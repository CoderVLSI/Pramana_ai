# Survey: sources for the remaining Upanishads of the Muktika 108

Work dir: `scratchpad/up/survey` (page images, djvu.xml, montages). Image index `nN` = `https://archive.org/download/<item>/page/nN_w1000.jpg`. Method: title pages located from the item's `_djvu.xml` (large-type headings), then verified by eye; offsets verified on 3+ pages per volume.

## 0. Headline

* **Best single source for ALL 108: Nirnaya Sagar "One Hundred & Eight Upanishads (Isha & others) with various readings", ed. Wasudev Laxman Shastri Pansikar, 3rd edition, Bombay (Pandurang Jawaji, Nirnaya-Sagar Press), Saka 1847 = 1925.** Text only, no commentary. This covers every one of the 81 slugs not already done (the 80 you counted, plus `atharvashiras-upanishad` if "narayanatharvashira" is not meant to cover it). Items (all the same book, different scans): `in.ernet.dli.2015.427659` (checked in detail), `in.ernet.dli.2015.320592` (identical file sizes), `tBgd_one-hundred-and-eight-upanishads-with-various-readings-edited-by-vasudev-laxman-` (NS 1925, Satya Vrat Shastri collection), `uDOS_one-hundred-and-eight-upanishads-with-various-readings-ed.-by-wasudev-laxman-sha` (1925, Pandurang Jawaji), `TppS_one-hundred-and-eight-upanishads-isha-and-others-ed-by-vasudev-lakshman-shastri-` (labelled 1917, probably 2nd ed.). A 4th ed. 1932 (`dli.ernet.427660`) is NOT pre-1931.
* **Second source (Adyar Library, commentary of Upanishad Brahmayogin), 74 of the 81**: Samanya Vedanta 1921, Vaishnava 1923, Saiva 1925, Yoga 1920, Samnyasa 1929. Useful for a second witness/different text-tradition and for the commentary, but the mantra text is interleaved with commentary (see section 2).
* **The 7 Sakta Upanishads (sita, devi, tripura, tripuratapini, bhavana, saubhagyalakshmi, sarasvatirahasya) are only in the Nirnaya Sagar 108 volume** among pre-1931 sources: the Adyar Sakta volume is 1950 (DLI `in.ernet.dli.2015.383591`, date 1950; the eGangotri scan `xefp-the-sakta-upanishads-pandit-a.-mahadeva-shastri` is the 1986 reprint of the 1950 ed.; its preface says "first edition 1925", editor's note dated 5 Nov 1925, but the 1929 Samnyasa preface lists only four Adyar vols by Sastri before it, and DLI dates it 1950). Treat as NOT PD unless a 1925-26 copy turns up (none found on archive.org).

* Units: verse/paragraph numbers ॥ १ ॥ restart in each Upanishad (some have khanda/adhyaya headings). Counts not tallied.

## 2. Layout of the Adyar volumes (checked n37-51, n62, n126-n147 etc. in SV; n28, n37, n47, n156, n202 in Saiva; n12, n76, n130, n250 in Vaishnava; n25-26 in Samnyasa; n46 in Yoga)
* Each Upanishad: large-type title, shanti, then the **mantras/prose are set in larger/bolder type in short blocks (about 3-15 verses each) with sub-headings in small italic ("विषय" titles)**; after each block follows a paragraph of **small-type commentary** (Upanishad-Brahmayogin: "इह खलु ... उपनिषत्", pratika-by-pratika gloss, with the verse range e.g. ॥ १-२ ॥). So text and commentary alternate; the text is NOT in a separate part. The Upanishad can be read continuously by concatenating the large-type blocks (verses are numbered ॥ १ ॥ ... continuous within each Upanishad; chapters headed खण्डः / अध्यायः), but the commentary re-quotes the mantra words in bold, so extraction must separate by type size. Hybrid risk: some pages are mostly commentary (e.g. SV n47, n253, n265).
* Variant readings: small footnotes with manuscript sigla (अ १, क, ङ ... ) - ignore.
* Maha Upanishad etc. have commentary that is far longer than the text (Maha: n272-n380). Vaishnava Nrisimhatapini n208-338 is mostly commentary.
* The Samnyasa and Yoga scans are higher resolution; SV/Vai/Saiva images are 500-ish px wide (n_w1000 gives ~500x800): readable but small; use the PDF/jp2 for hi-res.

## 4. Rights
* **Alladi/A. Mahadeva Sastri** (Director, Oriental section Adyar Library 1916-): died **1926** (family memoir, Star of Mysore "A Scholarly Orientalist from Mysuru Alladi Mahadeva Sastry": director from 1916 "till his death in 1926"; Online Books Page lists "Mahadeva Sastri, Alladi, -1926?"). Independent corroboration: the 1929 Samnyasa preface (n9 of that item) calls him "my predecessor the late Pandit A. Mahadeva Sastri". => Life+60 expired 1987 in India; vols of 1920/21/23/25 published pre-1931 => US public domain. **CONFIRMED for Yoga 1920, Samanya 1921, Vaishnava 1923, Saiva 1925** (death year rests on one family source + the "late" statement; so "confirmed" with the caveat that the exact year is 1926 per a memoir).
* **T. R. Chintamani (Dikshit)**, editor of Samnyasa 1929: death year NOT found (later Professor of Sanskrit, Madras University, editing as T. R. Chintamani M.A., Ph.D., Madras Univ. Sanskrit Series 1933-1939 and beyond). US: published 1929 => PD (US pre-1931 rule, in force as of 1 Jan 2026). India: **UNCONFIRMED** (life+60; editor's own contribution is limited to variant readings/notes, the Upanishads and Brahmayogin's 18th-c. commentary are ancient). Preface by C. Kunhan Raja (d. 1969) - not part of the text.
* **W. L. Shastri Pansikar** (NS 108, 1925): death year NOT found; Nirnaya Sagar/Pandurang Jawaji claim "all rights reserved by the publisher". US: published 1925 => PD. India: **UNCONFIRMED**. Only the editor's collation/footnotes/bhumika are his, the Upanishad texts are traditional.
* Adyar Sakta Upanishads: 1950 (reprinted 1966, 1986) => **not PD**; avoid.
* Pre-1931 US PD status holds for everything surveyed except the Sakta 1950 reprint and the 1932 NS 4th ed.
