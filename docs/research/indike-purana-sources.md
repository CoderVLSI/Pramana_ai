# Indike Pramana: what its Purana pages say about their sources (read 2026-11-09)

Method: `https://indike.org/pramana/in.purana.<work>?format=md` for the 18 Mahapuranas, only the metadata lines (Text / Extent / Source). No scripture text copied, no bulk download. Statements below are what the site says, not verified by us.

Finding: for every Purana except Vayu, the site's digital text comes from e-text projects (GRETIL, SARIT, Vedic Reserve, Sanskrit Documents, Sanskrit Wikisource). Our rules exclude GRETIL, Sanskrit Documents and Wikisource as text sources, and GRETIL/SARIT/Wikisource licences carry NC/SA conditions. So none of this text can be imported. What it gives us is edition leads.

## What to do with this
- Do NOT import any of it. Rights differ per source and several are excluded by our own rules.
- Use only as leads: Vayu Anandashrama 1905 (archive.org) as a second pre-1931 witness; Bombay Venkatesvara Press scans for Shiva, Linga, Garuda, Brahmanda (already in `docs/puranas-pilot/source-candidates.md` as candidates; check dates on the title pages).
- The site's `?format=md` pages work for reading metadata; the sources-and-licences page is JavaScript-rendered and was not readable here.
- Unresolved: the exact archive.org item id for the Vayu Anandashrama scan (the site names the edition, not the item id in the lines I read); whether the site would allow reuse of any text (needs written confirmation from its maintainer; draft message is in `indike-integration-plan.md`).
